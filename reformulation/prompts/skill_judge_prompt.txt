You are validating whether a paraphrased Web3 skill keyword preserves the same meaning as the original canonical skill keyword.

Return strict JSON only.
Do not include markdown.
Do not include explanations outside the JSON.
Do not add extra keys.

You will be given:

* skill_id
* original_skill_keyword
* skill_level
* skill_categories
* one_sentence_definition
* paraphrased_keyword

Scoring rubric:

meaning_preservation_score:
Score from 1 to 5.
5 means the paraphrase preserves the same skill meaning.
1 means the paraphrase changes the meaning or introduces a different skill.

specificity_score:
Score from 1 to 5.
5 means the paraphrase has the same level of specificity as the original skill.
1 means it is much too broad, too narrow, or changes the skill granularity.

lexical_quality_score:
Score from 1 to 5.
5 means the paraphrase is a clean, short keyword phrase.
1 means it is awkward, too long, sentence-like, unclear, or poorly formed.

non_expansion_score:
Score from 1 to 5.
5 means the paraphrase does not expand into a description, role, project-specific term, application area, or broader concept.
1 means it adds concepts beyond the original skill.

Validation rules:

* The paraphrase must not create a new skill.
* The paraphrase must not be broader than the original skill.
* The paraphrase must not be narrower than the original skill unless the narrowed meaning is already clearly part of the original keyword or definition.
* The paraphrase must not include project names, protocol names, brand names, or project-specific terms.
* The paraphrase must not include contributor roles such as educator, creator, advocate, tester, researcher, or community builder.
* The paraphrase should usually be 2 to 6 words.
* The paraphrase should be a keyword phrase, not a full sentence.
* If the original skill contains multiple important components, the paraphrase should preserve the combined meaning.

Return exactly this JSON schema:

{
"meaning_preservation_score": 5,
"specificity_score": 5,
"lexical_quality_score": 5,
"non_expansion_score": 5,
"is_new_skill": false,
"is_too_broad": false,
"is_too_narrow": false,
"is_project_specific": false,
"is_role_or_contributor_term": false,
"short_reason": "The paraphrase preserves the original skill meaning and remains a short keyword phrase."
}
